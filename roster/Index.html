How we calculate star progress

Record the full stars and the number of completed segments towards the next star. We keep them separate so a 3-star hero with four segments is not treated like a fresh 3-star hero.

Shard-cost verification is still required. Your research audit identifies inconsistent lifetime totals and counting conventions. The tool therefore does not invent an exact cost for every promotion. For the illustrative 3-star example, it shows the stated 40-shard segments and 100-shard final segment as an unverified working assumption, not a universal in-game rule.
Research basis and limitations

Hero roster and class classifications, generation mapping and selected skill evidence are derived from the user-supplied DRS Gen 1–17 audited workbook. Gen 5 Hector is explicitly identified as Lucky Wheel in the audit. Other acquisition routes remain unconfirmed rather than guessed. The audited report warns against declaring untested teams definitively best and against treating every Intel shard as guaranteed.

Future generation heroes are candidates for consideration, not automatic replacements. Standard Hero Gear is transferable and should not be treated as inherent hero strength. Specific star and weapon breakpoints need independent confirmation.
