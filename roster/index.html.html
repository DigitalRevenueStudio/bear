Bear Trap: leader vs joiner

These are different assessments. For a rally leader, review the three heroes’ relevant Expedition effects and troop setup. For a joining captain, check the selected first Expedition skill, its level and whether it is among the skills actually applied by the rally. Do not add the hero’s second/third skills or weapon effects as automatic joining buffs.

A skill list alone does not prove a hero is a better joiner. When skill ordering is not reliably established, the result is flagged for in-game checking.

Research and testing status

Verified: only independently corroborated source details, not battle performance. Provisional: single-source transcriptions or untested recommendations. Blocked: conflicting or incomplete figures. No win rates, best-team rankings, or future free shard income are invented.

To test on a phone, publish the HTML on your own HTTPS website; iOS file previews can restrict local scripts. This page runs offline after loading and does not transmit account data.

Decision safeguards

  • General Mythic shards should not automatically replace gems for an obtainable Lucky Wheel hero.
  • Jeronimo and Natalia cannot use general Mythic shards.
  • Bear Trap joiners contribute a selected first Expedition skill, not their entire Expedition kit.
  • Leader, joiner, Arena and garrison recommendations are different questions.
  • Lighthouse/Intel availability depends on the actual reward pool; a two-generation lag is not a guarantee for every hero.
  • Keep weapon effects, standard gear, stars and skill levels separate when comparing heroes.
