How to interpret this advisor

This is a working qualitative guide, not a battle simulator or a universal “best team” calculator. Recommendations use your declared role gaps, activity, owned heroes and current performance. Generation and stars alone do not prove a crossover. Current-lineup flags help identify same-job comparators; they do not claim an optimised team.

Keep Arena healing, control, summons and Escorts separate from troop combat. Leaders use a full Expedition kit; joiners contribute only an eligible selected first skill. Actual rally selection and stacking still require alliance/client checks. A maximum proc is not permanent output, and Attack, Lethality, Defence and damage modifiers are not interchangeable.

Wheel badges identify the featured hero at release, not guaranteed current acquisition. Starter first-skill details are incomplete for some early Mythics; their profile links are provided instead of invented values. Specialist sources and account availability can change.

Zinman: the 60/40/0 Infantry/Lancer garrison idea is community-reported specialist guidance, not proven universal consensus or a guaranteed lifespan. Gwen: practical Bear-joining first-skill behaviour is disputed, so she is held from automatic joining recommendations.

No roster is sent to a server. Browser storage is convenient, not a backup guarantee; export your roster. The app uses no external libraries or network requests. Published profile links open only when clicked.
