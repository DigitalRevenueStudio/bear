Evidence and limitations

Recommendations combine the user-audited Gen 1–17 research with published acquisition schedules and independent community investment guides. They are editorial investment judgments, not verified controlled-match win-rate rankings. Confidence varies by hero. Skill transcription counts are not proof of strength.

A hero's long-term usefulness depends on activity, stars, widgets, troops, and later balance changes. This advisor names future targets where sources support them; it does not invent guaranteed competitive lifespans. Wheel rotations beyond Gen 12 are not confirmed by the primary published schedule.
