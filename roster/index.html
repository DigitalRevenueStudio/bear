Research and limitations

“High priority” is attributed editorial role guidance, not a statistical proof that a hero beats your current build. “Skip” means skip THIS objective/project, not every use. A later Wheel window is a review date, not predicted lifespan. Current targets do not model all base stats, troop composition, gear or exclusive-skill scaling.

Exact general-shard eligibility is enforced: recruited Legendary only, excluding Natalia/Jeronimo. Random Wheel rewards are never treated as guaranteed. Wheel planning uses published 1500 single / 13500 ten-spin pricing, milestone thresholds 5/15/35/70/120, and 0.5466 expected random shards per spin. Verify event rules and prices on your state. Recruitment shards are reserved before treating dedicated income as promotion stock.

Open conflicts include skill/weapon transcriptions, inconsistent release dates and shard-total conventions. Numeric ambiguities stay quarantined in inherited data rather than repaired by guessing. Simulator evidence carries its assumptions; for example the Gen12 model has explicit gear, tier and joiner assumptions and incomplete skill registrations.
