4. Generated setups

Selection rules and limitations

Lead draft score: 40 × (stars + segments/6) + 4 × total Expedition levels + 2 × generation + 10 for Legendary + editorial Bear fit. Fit: Jeronimo/Hector/Mia +40; Bradley/Blanchette/Aisling/Eleanor +20; Jessie/Jasser/Seo-yoon/Gina/Patrick/Walis Bokan/Zinman −40; others 0. Trio score also adds 0.15 × available reviewed joiner scores. These are prototype editorial weights, NOT measured damage. Override the lead trio if you have a tested team.

Joiner score prioritises first-skill level, then an editorial preference for damage, Lethality, Attack and enemy-Defence reduction, then magnitude. These stats are NOT mathematically interchangeable. Stars/weapon do not rank joining skills; capacity still needs manual confirmation. Joining captains are reserved before distinct-class filler heroes. No hero repeats.

Reviewed: Jessie, Jasser, Jeronimo, Seo-yoon, Bradley, Blanchette, Aisling, Bertha, Eleanor and Gregory. Complete leader kits, all other joining effects, gear, troop tiers, skill unlock gates, actual capacity, travel times and exact damage remain outside the model. No guaranteed four-slot selection is implied.
