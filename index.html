4. Your generated setups

How selection works — read before using live

Leader draft score per hero: 40 × (full stars + completed segments/6) + 4 × sum of Expedition skill levels + 2 × generation + 10 for Legendary rarity + editorial Bear fit. Jeronimo/Hector/Mia fit +40; Bradley/Blanchette/Aisling/Eleanor +20; Jessie/Jasser/Seo-yoon/Gina/Patrick/Walis Bokan/Zinman −40; others 0. Trio selection adds 0.15 × the available reviewed joiner scores to help retain joining options. These weights are a visible editorial prototype, NOT measured damage. Unreviewed heroes can be selected on development alone. Override the trio to use your tested leaders.

Joiners: reviewed first skills only, prioritised by skill level, then an editorial preference for damage, Lethality, Attack, enemy Defence reduction, then skill magnitude. This is NOT a mathematical equivalence between different stats. Joiner scores ignore stars and weapon levels because this step ranks the joining skill contribution, not deployment capacity. All joining captains are reserved before filler heroes are assigned. No hero is used twice.

Reviewed first-skill profiles: Jessie, Jasser, Seo-yoon, Jeronimo, Bradley, Blanchette, Aisling, Bertha, Eleanor and Gregory. Other hero names through Gen 17 are selectable for leader drafts/filler slots, but their first-slot joining effects are not yet implemented. Unreviewed does not mean unsuitable.

Missing: complete hero-specific leader skill model, gear/stats, troop tiers, time, star-skill unlock validation, capacity from heroes, exact damage, four-slot selection against other players and every first-skill profile. Troops-only fallback is a review prompt, not guaranteed deployment capacity. Check the yellow-flag skill panel and follow alliance instructions.
